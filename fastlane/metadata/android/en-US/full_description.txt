CleanShare helps you share files safely. Rename files with custom names and strip sensitive metadata — like EXIF data in photos — before sending them to anyone.

Features:
• Rename files before sharing
• Remove metadata to protect your privacy
• Simple, fast interface built for quick file prep
• Handles files shared into the app from anywhere

Everything is processed on-device. Your files never leave your phone: no accounts, no ads, no tracking, and no internet connection required.
